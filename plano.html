App subscription + two standalone content packs

How to structure products, prices, and discounts in Stripe for a recurring core app plus two one-time content add-ons — built for the same customer to buy all three.

01

The three products

The app is the subscription that carries recurring revenue. Content A and Content B are standalone purchases — paid once, no future billing — and act as a natural upsell once the customer already trusts the app.

02

How to model this in Stripe

One Product and one Price per item — never a second Price with the discount baked in. The list price stays fixed; the discount layered on top is what moves the number.

Stripe objectCore AppContent A / B
Price recurring, $19/month one-time, $29 and $17
Checkout mode subscription payment
Coupon none fixed amount off, restricted to the Product via applies_to.products
Promotion Code — a code on top of the coupon — turns the promo on/off without touching the price

Why not just create a second Price at $22 and $12: a discounted Price becomes a second source of truth for pricing, breaks revenue reporting, and can't be turned off without editing the catalog. A coupon solves this — toggle it, expire it, or cap its redemptions without touching the original Price.

04

Open decisions

Points worth answering before implementing — they change the checkout flow above.

  • Is content access lifetime?A one-time purchase usually implies permanent ownership. If it's actually a 1-year access window instead, that item becomes an annual Subscription, not a one-time Price.
  • Is the discount always visible, or a one-off promo?Decides whether the reduced price shows directly on the page (apply the coupon automatically in the Checkout Session) or the customer has to type a code (allow_promotion_codes: true).
  • Does the discount depend on already owning the app?If it's a conditional upsell — "since you're already a subscriber, get Content A for $22" — the Promotion Code needs to be generated dynamically at the right moment, not left public.
  • Does canceling the app affect access to content already bought?A Checkout Session in payment mode is a one-off charge — it isn't tied to the app's Subscription object, so canceling the subscription has no effect on it by default. Confirm that's the intent.
