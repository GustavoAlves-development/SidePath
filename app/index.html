Scripts & templates

The exact wording for the four moments that trip people up most. Copy, fill in the brackets, send.

Cold outreach message

Hi [Name], I noticed [specific detail about their business or post]. I help [what you do] for people like you — [one-line proof or result]. Would you be open to a quick chat this week?

Price quote message

Here's what I'd propose: [scope in one sentence] for $[price], delivered by [date]. That includes [what's included]. I can start as soon as you confirm.

Referral ask

Glad this worked out for you! If you know one or two people who could use the same thing, I'd really appreciate the introduction — that's how most of my work comes together.

Late payment follow-up

Hi [Name], just a friendly check-in — the invoice for [job] was due on [date] and I haven't seen it come through yet. Could you confirm when I should expect it? Happy to resend if that's easier.

[Item name] — [condition, one honest detail]. Selling because [brief real reason]. Priced to move, message with any questions.

Negotiation response

I can do $[slightly lower number] if you can pick up by [date] — that's the best I can do on this one.

Booking confirmation

Confirmed for [date/time] — see you then! Reply here anytime if anything changes on your end.

Cancellation policy notice

Just so it's clear upfront: reschedules need 24 hours notice, and same-day cancellations have a $[fee] fee.

Testimonial request

So glad it worked out! Would you be up for a quick two-line review of how it went? It really helps other people trust the process.

Re-engaging an old lead

Hi [Name], circling back after a while — still working on [problem they had]? Happy to pick this back up if the timing's better now.

Discovery question opener

Quick question before I quote this — what's the actual deadline you're working against, and what happens if it slips?

Scope-creep boundary

Happy to add that — it's outside the original scope, so here's a quick add-on quote: $[amount] for [what's added].

Everything's wrapped up on my end — let me know if anything needs a final tweak, otherwise consider this one done. Great working with you!
